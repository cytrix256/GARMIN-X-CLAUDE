# 001 — Weekly training review

**Status** — draft, ready to build
**Roadmap item** — Now #1, the demo

## Problem

The dashboard shows everything and says nothing. Six tabs of charts assume the
reader already knows which chart to look at and what a bad value looks like.
That is the opposite of what the paid product does: a subscription tells you
what to think about this week.

The reader is strength-dominant, trains ~3x/week, and will not open a dashboard
out of habit. See `customers/me.md`.

## Output

One markdown file per run at `demos/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-review.md`, generated by a
new `weekly_review.py`, covering the seven days ending on the run date.

Sections, in this order, each one short:

1. **The week in one line.** What happened, stated plainly. No score.
2. **What you did.** Sessions by sport, total load, and how that compares to the
   four weeks before it. Direction matters more than absolute numbers.
3. **Where that leaves you.** Fitness, fatigue and form from `fitness_fatigue`,
   plus the acute:chronic ratio with its band named rather than just plotted.
4. **Recovery and sleep.** Recovery score trend, sleep debt, HRV against the
   personal band — each with the components visible, not just the composite.
5. **One thing worth noticing.** The single strongest item from
   `analytics.insights()`, quoted with its `n` and correlation, and written so a
   weak signal reads as weak.
6. **What is missing.** Days with no data in the window, and any populated
   `failures`. A gap that is stated is not a bug; a gap that is hidden is.

Markdown, not HTML. The dashboard is the visual artefact; this is the one that
gets read.

## Inputs available

Everything from `analytics.build()`: `fitness_fatigue`, `acwr`, `monotony`,
`hrv`, `recovery`, `sleep_debt`, `insights`, `scatter`, `weekday`. Plus
`data/activities.json` for the session list and `data/garmin_data.json` for
`daily`, `ranges` and `failures`. Checked against `context/data.md` — no new
fetching required.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `python weekly_review.py` writes `demos/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-review.md` and exits 0.
- [ ] It runs offline, from committed data, with no new dependencies.
- [ ] Every number in the output traces to a function in `analytics.py` or a
      field in `data/`. Nothing is estimated, rounded into meaning, or asserted.
- [ ] A week with no activities produces a sensible file, not a crash and not a
      fabricated summary.
- [ ] Where a correlation is quoted, `n` is quoted beside it.
- [ ] No sentence recommends an action the data cannot support. "Your form is
      negative and falling" is in scope; "take a rest day" is not, yet.
- [ ] I read the generated file end to end and would read the next one.

That last one is the real test. The others are how it fails cheaply before
reaching it.

## Non-goals

- Coaching, prescriptions, or a plan for next week. That is the Later column.
- HTML, styling, or a link from the dashboard. Markdown in `demos/` is enough.
- Scheduling it. `routines/weekly-review.md` covers running it by hand. Automate
  after it has run twice and survived.
- Anything multi-user.

## Open questions

- **What is a good week?** Nothing in the data says what this reader is training
  toward, so the review describes rather than judges. Assumption for v1:
  describe, compare to the previous four weeks, and let the reader judge.
- **Strength-dominant training under endurance models.** `activityTrainingLoad`
  exists per activity, so load is at least Garmin's own number rather than
  invented — but the Banister taus and the ACWR bands were not built for a
  46-of-73-sessions strength profile. Assumption for v1: use them, and say so in
  section 3 rather than quietly presenting them as tuned. Flagged in
  `customers/me.md` as the thing to resolve properly.
- **Seven days ending when?** Assumption: the run date, not calendar weeks, so
  the routine can be run on any day and still make sense.
